# EX. No.3.1. CREATE 3D MODEL FROM ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION
## DATE:02-11-2026

## AIM:
To draw the three orthographic views (Top View, Front View
and Right Side View) of the given mechanical component using
the given dimensions and suitable engineering drawing
instruments.
The main objective of this experiment is to understand the
orthographic projection method and represent a
three-dimensional mechanical object in two-dimensional views.
The drawing helps to identify the shape, size, holes, curved
surfaces, slots and other important features of the component.
The three views are drawn with proper alignment, dimensions,
centre lines, visible lines and hidden lines to obtain an accurate
engineering drawing.

## REQUIREMENTS:
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload

## PROCEDURE:

Step 1: Study the given drawing<br>

● First, carefully observe the given engineering drawing.<br>
● Identify the three views: Top View, Front View and Right Side
View.<br>
● Note the dimensions, holes, slots, rectangular portions and
curved surfaces.<br>
● Understand the position of each view and their relationship.<br>
● Select a suitable scale, preferably 1:1 if the drawing sheet
permits.<br>

<img width="1035" height="722" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f44fa8a4-0161-4ccc-9e23-acca052c7b2d" /><br>

Step 2: Draw the Front View<br>

● First, draw a horizontal reference line for the Front View.<br>
● Mark the centre point of the rounded portion.<br>
● Draw a semicircular end using a radius of 16 mm.<br>
● Draw a circular hole of diameter 16 mm at the centre of the
rounded end.<br>
● Extend the horizontal lines to form the rectangular main body.<br>
● Mark the distance of 36 mm from the hole centre to the
shoulder.<br>
● Draw the extended rectangular portion with a length of 32 mm.<br>
● Draw the vertical boundary lines to complete the main body.<br>
● Add the centre line through the circular hole.<br>
● Draw the hidden lines to represent the internal edges.<br>

Step 3: Draw the Top View<br>

● Draw the Top View above the Front View.<br>
● Project the required vertical construction lines from the Front
View.<br>
● Mark the overall width as 40 mm.<br>
● Divide the width into three portions: 11 mm, 18 mm and 11 mm.<br>
● Draw the upper and lower rectangular portions.<br>
● Construct the central open slot with a width of 18 mm<br>
● Mark the horizontal dimensions according to the given drawing.<br>
● Draw the extended rectangular portion on the right side.<br>
● Represent the hidden edges using dashed lines.<br>
● Draw the necessary centre lines to indicate the position of the
holes.<br>

Step 4: Draw the Right Side View<br>

● Draw the Right Side View on the right side of the Front View.<br>
● Project the necessary horizontal lines from the Front View.<br>
● Draw the outer rectangular boundary with a width of 40 mm and
a height of 32 mm.<br>
● Mark the centre of the view.<br>
● Draw the circular hole with a diameter of 18 mm.<br>
● Draw the horizontal and vertical centre lines passing through
the circular hole.<br>
● Represent the internal edges using hidden lines.<br>
● Complete the Right Side View according to the given
dimensions.<br>

<img width="1007" height="476" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/616d38fa-6c0e-48ec-81a4-9f2a6ca0e5dd" /><br>

Step 5: Finishing the Drawing<br>

● Check the alignment of all three orthographic views.<br>
● Darken the visible outlines using a suitable pencil.<br>
● Draw hidden edges using dashed lines.<br>
● Draw centre lines using thin chain lines.<br>
● Add all necessary dimensions, arrows and extension lines.<br>
● Erase unwanted construction lines.<br>
● Check the accuracy and neatness of the completed drawing.<br>
